Dynamics of Collective-Dephasing-Induced Multi-Atom Entanglement

Authors: Y. Li, Y. Mei, H. Nguyen, P. R. Berman, and A. Kuzmich
Reference: Phys. Rev. A 106, L051701 (2022) & Supplemental Material

1. Collective Operators and the Symmetric Dicke Subspace

For an ensemble of \(N\) identical two-level atoms with ground state \(|g\rangle\) and Rydberg state \(|r\rangle\), for atom index \(j \in \{1, 2, \dots, N\}\):

  • Single-atom transition operators: $$\hat{\sigma}_{rg}^{(j)} = |r\rangle_j \langle g|_j, \quad \hat{\sigma}_{gr}^{(j)} = |g\rangle_j \langle r|_j$$ $$\hat{\sigma}_z^{(j)} = |r\rangle_j \langle r|_j - |g\rangle_j \langle g|_j$$
  • Collective angular momentum operators: $$\hat{J}_z = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^N \hat{\sigma}_z^{(j)}, \quad \hat{J}_+ = \sum_{j=1}^N \hat{\sigma}_{rg}^{(j)}, \quad \hat{J}_- = \sum_{j=1}^N \hat{\sigma}_{gr}^{(j)}$$ $$\hat{J}_x = \frac{\hat{J}_+ + \hat{J}_-}{2}, \quad \hat{J}_y = \frac{\hat{J}_+ - \hat{J}_-}{2i}$$
  • Commutation relations: $$[\hat{J}_x, \hat{J}_y] = i \hat{J}_z, \quad [\hat{J}_y, \hat{J}_z] = i \hat{J}_x, \quad [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] = i \hat{J}_y$$
  • Symmetric Dicke basis \(\{|n\rangle\}_{n=0}^N\): $$|n\rangle \equiv \left|J = \frac{N}{2}, M = n - \frac{N}{2}\right\rangle = \sqrt{\frac{n!(N-n)!}{N!}} \sum_{\pi \in S_N} \pi \left( |r\rangle^{\otimes n} \otimes |g\rangle^{\otimes (N-n)} \right)$$

2. Initial State: Unentangled Rydberg Spin-Wave

A weak excitation pulse of duration \(T_p\) creates an uncorrelated product state across all \(N\) atoms:

$$|\psi(0)\rangle = \bigotimes_{j=1}^N \left( \cos\frac{\theta}{2} |g\rangle_j + \sin\frac{\theta}{2} e^{i \mathbf{k} \cdot \mathbf{r}_j} |r\rangle_j \right) = \sum_{n=0}^N c_n |n\rangle$$

where the excitation probability is \(p_r = \sin^2(\theta/2) \ll 1\), and the coefficients are:

$$c_n = \sqrt{\binom{N}{n}} \left(\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\right)^{N-n} \left(\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\right)^n \approx e^{-\bar{n}/2} \frac{\bar{n}^{n/2}}{\sqrt{n!}}$$
  • Vacuum (\(n=0\)): \(c_0 \approx e^{-\bar{n}/2} \quad (|0\rangle = |g \dots g\rangle)\)
  • Single-excitation Dicke state (\(n=1\)): \(c_1 \approx \sqrt{\bar{n}} e^{-\bar{n}/2} \quad \left(|1\rangle = |W\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{j=1}^N |g \dots r_j \dots g\rangle\right)\)
  • Two-excitation state (\(n=2\)): \(c_2 \approx \frac{\bar{n}}{\sqrt{2}} e^{-\bar{n}/2}\)

3. Rydberg Interaction & Interaction-Induced Dephasing

The pairwise Rydberg interaction Hamiltonian during storage time \(T_s\) is:

$$\hat{H}_{\text{int}} = \sum_{1 \le j < l \le N} V_{jl} |r\rangle_j |r\rangle_l \langle r|_j \langle r|_l, \quad V_{jl} = \frac{C_6}{|\mathbf{r}_j - \mathbf{r}_l|^6}$$
The Key Selective Dephasing Mechanism:
  • \(\hat{H}_{\text{int}} |0\rangle = 0\)
  • \(\hat{H}_{\text{int}} |1\rangle = 0\) — Zero interaction shift! A single Rydberg atom has no partner to interact with.
  • \(\hat{H}_{\text{int}} |n \ge 2\rangle \neq 0\) — Multi-excitation coherences dephase at rate \(\Gamma_n \propto \frac{n(n-1)}{2} \gamma_{\text{int}}\).

The coherences evolve as:

$$\rho_{00}(t) = |c_0|^2, \quad \rho_{11}(t) = |c_1|^2 e^{-\gamma_s t}, \quad \rho_{01}(t) = c_0 c_1^* e^{-\frac{1}{2}\gamma_s t}$$ $$\rho_{nn'}(t) = c_n c_{n'}^* \exp\left[ -\frac{1}{2}(\Gamma_n + \Gamma_{n'}) t - \frac{n + n'}{2}\gamma_s t \right] \quad (n, n' \ge 2)$$

4. Second-Order Autocorrelation Function \(g^{(2)}(T_s)\) & Scaling Ansatz

From Eq. (1) of the paper, the phase-matched photon correlation function is:

$$g^{(2)}(T_s) = \frac{\sum_{m \ge 2} |c_m|^2 m(m - 1) X_m(T_s)}{\left| \sum_{m \ge 1} |c_m|^2 m Y_m(T_s) \right|^2}$$

Using the crucial scaling ansatz (Supplemental Material Eqs. S.7–S.12):

$$X_m = \frac{(N - m)^2 + 3(N - m)}{N^2} \left( \frac{X_2 - 2/N^2}{1 - 1/N - 2/N^2} \right)^{2m - 3} + \frac{2}{N^2}$$ $$Y_m = \frac{N - m}{N} \left( \frac{Y_2 - 1/N}{1 - 2/N} \right)^{m - 1} + \frac{1}{N}$$

In the large-\(N\) limit (\(N \gg m\)):

$$X_m \approx X_2^{2m - 3}, \quad Y_m \approx Y_2^{m - 1}$$

where the two-body quantities scale as \(N^2\):

$$X_2(T_s) = \frac{1}{N^4} \left| \sum_{\mu=1}^N \sum_{\nu=1}^N \exp(-i \Phi_{\mu\nu}) \right|^2, \quad Y_2(T_s) = \frac{1}{N^3} \sum_{\mu=1}^N \left| \sum_{\nu=1}^N \exp(-i \Phi_{\mu\nu}) \right|^2$$

5. Pairwise Concurrence Dynamics (Entanglement Emergence)

The two-atom reduced density matrix in the basis \(\{|gg\rangle, |gr\rangle, |rg\rangle, |rr\rangle\}\) yields the Wootters concurrence:

$$C(\rho_{12}) = 2 \max\left( 0, \frac{p_1(t)}{N} - \sqrt{p_0(t) \frac{2 p_2(t)}{N(N - 1)}} \right)$$
Universal Scaled Concurrence (\(N \gg 1\)): $$N \times C(t) = 2 \max\left( 0, p_1(t) - \sqrt{2 p_0(t) p_2(t)} \right)$$
  • At \(t = 0\): \(\sqrt{2 p_0(0) p_2(0)} = p_1(0) \implies C(0) = 0\) (strictly unentangled initial state).
  • For \(t > 0\): As Rydberg dephasing suppresses \(p_2(t) \to 0\), concurrence rises to an optimal positive peak: $$N \times C(t) \approx 2 \bar{n} e^{-\bar{n}} \left( 1 - e^{-\Gamma_2 t / 2} \right) > 0$$ proving interaction-induced entanglement generation!

6. Antibunching, Dicke State Purity, and Quantum Fisher Information

  • Second-order intensity correlation: $$g^{(2)}(t) = \frac{2 p_2(t)}{p_1(t)^2} = g^{(2)}(0) e^{-\Gamma_2 t} \xrightarrow{t \to \infty} 0 \quad \text{(single-photon emission)}$$
  • Dicke state purity: $$\mathcal{P}_{\text{Dicke}}(t) = \frac{p_1(t)}{1 - p_0(t)} \xrightarrow{t \to \infty} 1.0 \quad \text{(filters into pure } |W\rangle \text{ state)}$$
  • Quantum Fisher Information: $$\frac{F_Q(t)}{N} \approx 4 p_1(t) \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2} g^{(2)}(t) \right) > 1 \quad \text{(surpasses Standard Quantum Limit)}$$
